WHAT WE DO

Specialized AD security. Real-world results.

Every engagement follows the same attack chain a real adversary would use — then shows you exactly how to detect and stop it.

Active Directory Assessments

Comprehensive AD security evaluations to discover vulnerabilities and misconfigurations before attackers do.

Red Team Engagements

Simulated attacks that expose real-world risks in your AD environment, run under controlled, authorized conditions.

Attack Path Analysis

Identify the exact chain of misconfigurations an attacker could use to escalate privileges to Domain Admin.

Security Reporting & Remediation

Clear, prioritized reports with actionable recommendations to reduce risk — no jargon, no fluff.

Continuous Validation

Re-test and verify that remediations are effective and risks stay reduced over time, not just at the point-in-time report.

OUR TEAM

Four specialists. One attack chain.

Each of us owns a different stage of the engagement, from breaching the domain to detecting and reporting the breach. Click a role to see what they do.

AD / RED TEAM

Matyas Abreham

Red Team Lead

Leads offensive operations against the domain — enumerates the environment, chains misconfigurations together, and proves exactly how far an attacker could actually get.

  • Credential attacks & privilege escalation
  • Lateral movement across the network
  • Full attack-path documentation
WINDOWS / AD

Umer

Infrastructure Engineer

Builds and hardens the Active Directory environment our red team attacks — designing realistic, intentionally-flawed enterprise infrastructure to test against.

  • Infrastructure hardening guidance
